## Product Overview

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Boutique, independent, and lifestyle hotels (roughly 10–150 keys); owner-operated or small groups. Often under-marketed, over-reliant on OTAs, and lacking a dedicated commercial/revenue function.
**Decision-makers:** Hotel owner, GM, or Director of Operations. Sometimes a hands-on owner wearing every hat.
**Primary use case:** Grow revenue and reduce OTA dependency without hiring a full commercial team.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Increase RevPAR / direct-booking share
- Improve guest experience and reviews
- Get expert operator help without a full-time exec hire
**Use cases:** Revenue/commercial audits, direct-booking strategy, distribution/OTA optimization, guest-experience improvement, pre-opening/repositioning support.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Independent/boutique hotels compete against chains and OTAs without the commercial teams, systems, or expertise those bigger players have — so they leave revenue on the table and stay dependent on OTA channels.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Big consulting firms are expensive and not boutique-native
- Generic marketing agencies don't understand hotel commercial ops
- OTAs "help" but take margin and own the guest relationship
- DIY leaves owners guessing
**What it costs them:** Lost RevPAR, OTA commission bleed, weak direct channel, owner burnout, missed guest-experience revenue.
**Emotional tension:** Feeling outgunned by chains, frustration at OTA dependency, the loneliness of running a property with no expert in your corner.

**Key differentiators:**

- Boutique-native — built for independent/lifestyle hotels, not enterprise chains
- Commercial + experience + direct-booking focus in one advisor
- Productized "Tier 0" resources (revenue projections, visibility & discoverability audits, outreach playbooks) that deliver value fast and open engagements
**How we solve it differently:** Practical, operator-to-operator, data-backed, and focused on reducing OTA dependency via direct channels.
**Why that's better:** Faster trust, more relevant playbooks, and a direct-booking outcome the OTAs can't offer.
**Why clients choose us:** Real operating experience + boutique focus + a clear commercial plan.

## Objections & Anti-Personas
**Top objections:**
1. "Can't I just use my OTA rep / do it myself?" → OTAs optimize for OTAs; show the direct-booking upside and margin recovery.
2. "Is a consultant worth the cost?" → Frame vs. revenue lift and commission saved; lead with a low-risk audit.
3. "Do you understand *my* kind of property?" → Boutique-native positioning + operator background.
**Anti-persona:** Large branded/chain hotels with in-house commercial teams; hostels/budget properties competing purely on price.

## Switching Dynamics
- **Push:** OTA commission pain, flat revenue, bad reviews, owner overwhelm.
- **Pull:** Operator expertise, a concrete plan, direct-booking growth, quick-win audits.
- **Habit:** Reliance on OTAs and "the way we've always done it."
- **Anxiety:** "Will this actually pay off?" "Is it worth my time?" "Another vendor to manage."
